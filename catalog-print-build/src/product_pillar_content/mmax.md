WHAT IT IS
A two-component methyl methacrylate (MMA) resin coating with corundum aggregate. Squeegee-, roller-, or spray-applied. Used as a coloured lane treatment for transit lanes, BRT corridors, and cycle priority zones — anywhere a fast-curing, high-grip, fade-resistant coloured surface is needed.

Not a preformed thermoplastic. MMAX is liquid-applied and cures via a BPO (benzoyl peroxide) catalyst added at installation.

KEY FEATURES
- Cures to traffic-ready in 30 to 60 minutes — typically 45 minutes — enabling complete overnight installation in active corridors.
- Corundum aggregate (9 Mohs hardness — hardest in the anti-skid lineup) embedded throughout the cured film.
- Extended Season variant applies down to 35°F (2°C) substrate temperature and up to 150°F (66°C).
- VOC < 100 g/L (calculated) for the Extended Season formulation.
- EF Green colour meets FHWA chromaticity coordinates for bike-lane green; Transit Lane Red meets coordinates for bus-lane red.
- ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturing.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- Material: Two-component MMA resin + corundum aggregate. BPO catalyst added at install.
- Aggregate hardness: Corundum, 9 Mohs
- Density: 18.5 ± 0.5 lbs/gallon (ASTM D1475)
- Viscosity: 85–105 Krebs
- Shore hardness: 50–60 Shore D (ASTM D2240)
- Elongation: > 30% (ASTM D638 Type I)
- Total solids: > 99%
- Water absorption: < 0.25% (ASTM D570)
- VOC: < 100 g/L (Extended Season)
- Build thickness: 90 mil typical per kit coverage spec (~2.3 mm)
- Application temperature: 35°F to 150°F (2°C to 66°C) — Extended Season variant
- Pot life after BPO: < 15 minutes
- No-pickup time: < 30 minutes at 77°F (25°C) per ASTM D711
- Cure to traffic: 30–60 minutes (temperature + catalyst dependent)
- Skid resistance: > 60 BPN per ASTM E303 (Extended Season with corundum)
- Coverage: 45–50 sq ft per kit at 90-mil build (one kit = 2.79 gal mixed)
- Shelf life: 1 year unopened, stored 40°F–80°F

PALETTE
8 standard kit colours: EF Green (PMS 361C), Transit Lane Red (PMS 7622C), Truffle (PMS 7530C), Terracotta (PMS 7595C), Brick Red (PMS 7624C), Hollywood Green (PMS 7484C), Red (PMS 200C), White. Additional EF Black, EF Yellow, EF Blue, EF Bus Lane Red. Custom available.

APPLICATIONS
- Coloured bike lanes (EF Green)
- Coloured bus lanes / BRT corridors (Transit Lane Red)
- Transit priority surfaces and bus-stop pads
- Coloured crosswalks where rapid cure is required
- Symbols and legends on coloured background lanes
- Cool-weather and shoulder-season work (Extended Season variant)
