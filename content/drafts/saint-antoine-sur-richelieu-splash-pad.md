On the splash pad in Saint-Antoine-sur-Richelieu, Québec, islands the colour of sand lie in a field of sky blue, each edged in a deeper blue, and the colour comes from [StreetBond](/products/streetbond) pavement coating. Spray features stand over it, among them a palm tree with green and yellow fronds and a parasol in blue and green. The pad is at the town's community centre, and its photograph is in HUB Surface Systems' Product Portfolio Book · Volume 5, beside the book's page on splash pads.

## A design drawn in coating

Every shape on the pad is a colour change in the one surface. In the photograph the islands' outlines curve and swell, the colour runs right up to the foot of each spray feature, and the pad's outer edge meets the lawn in one smooth curve. The pad is dry in the picture, with a black pergola at its far side and a playground in view to the right.

StreetBond comes in a standard range of colours, and the book's colour pages note that custom Pantone matching is available, so a design can bring colours of its own.

## What the pad asks of the coating

The book's spread asks for "a slip-resistant texture", and that is how StreetBond goes onto a pad, in a textured build. The spread specifies it as UV-stable, and its printed service life is 8+ years. Wear is dealt with by recoating, between seasons. For a pad that should run cooler under bare feet, the spread's second system is [StreetBondSR](/products/streetbondsr), whose colours reflect more of the sun.

The spread is the eighth of the book's applications, on [pages 60 and 61](/product-book/60), and this pad is the picture it uses. The site's [splash pads page](/applications/splash-pads) describes the coating's build for a pad in more detail.
