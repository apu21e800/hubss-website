In HUB Surface Systems' Product Portfolio Book · Volume 5, Woodstock, Ontario, appears under a six-word headline: "Parking lanes with a subtle accent." The accent is [StreetPrint stamped asphalt](/products/streetprint) in a custom pattern, on the parking lanes of Dundas Street downtown, and the page gives the rest in one sentence: "A custom pattern was used to complete this StreetPrint project in the Woodstock downtown revitalization."

## A grey pattern across from City Hall

The photograph on [page 96 of the book](/product-book/96) is taken low over the parking lane, and the pattern in the foreground is light grey: brick shapes in small groups, each group at right angles to the ones beside it, with plain squares set among them. Beyond it the travel lanes are plain black asphalt with a yellow centre line, and City Hall stands across the street, a stone building with a clock tower. The page sits under the eyebrow Streetscapes, one of the uses printed along the foot of StreetPrint's own page.

## How the pattern goes into the street

Page 20 of the book sets out StreetPrint's method. Infrared heaters warm existing asphalt until it can be worked and a flexible template presses the pattern in while it is hot; [StreetBond](/products/streetbond) pavement coating then colours and seals the surface. New asphalt can take a pattern too, since StreetPrint's page lists its base as "New or existing asphalt". Each stage has its own section in [the guide to installing stamped asphalt](/blog/how-stamped-asphalt-is-installed).

The templates are on pages 22 and 23 of the book, the standard field and border patterns first and then the line "Custom templates available on request."

## Flush with the street around it

The pattern is pressed into the asphalt itself, so the lane stays flush with the street. A plow blade, by the book's account on StreetPrint's page (page 19), finds nothing to catch on, and there are no joints to weed or pavers to settle. The asphalt under the pattern stays flexible. The book puts StreetPrint's service life at 10–20 years and gives the StreetBond coat that carries the colour 8+ years, easily refreshed.
