## Shot guidance

- Civic plaza, laneway, or town square with decorative surface treatment
- DecoMark public art integration, StreetBond colour blocking, or StreetPrint texture
- Show people using the space — benches, gathering, community feel
- Indigenous art crosswalks, pride crosswalks, or mural-integrated pavement all qualify
