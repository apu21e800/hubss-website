Walk a lot or a street in October and the list is usually short: a crack across a drive aisle, a joint opening where the asphalt meets a curb, and a small pothole by the entrance. Each is still a small job, and an asphalt crack filler or a patch will do it before the first freeze.

Over a Canadian winter, water turns each one into a bigger job. It runs into the crack, freezes and pushes the edges apart, and every thaw lets more in, until by spring the crack is a broken edge or a hole. An open joint lets water under the pavement on both sides of it. A small pothole holds water, and tires and plow blades break its edges until it is not small any more.

## Asphalt crack filler for the crack and the open joint

[ChipFill](/products/chipfill)'s product page names what it is best for: smaller potholes, cracks and joints. It is thermoplastic in small chips. They are poured in, levelled and heated with a propane torch, and once heated they conform to the shape of the damage and bond to the surrounding asphalt or concrete, sealing out the water that drives freeze-thaw damage. It needs no specialized equipment and no hot-mix plant, it can go in year-round in any weather, and the lane reopens to traffic within minutes.

The page lists edge joints among its uses, with patrol patching, emergency repairs and utility cuts, on roads, parking lots, sidewalks and pathways. Where the joint runs along a concrete slab whose edge has spalled, [Fast Patch DPR](/products/fast-patch) is the product whose page names both spalls and joints, in asphalt and in concrete.

## The small pothole, and the one that is not small

ChipFill's page leads with potholes: thermoplastic chips for pothole repair, heated in place, year-round and in all weather. A small pothole takes it the same way a crack does, and traffic is back within minutes.

Where the hole is too deep or wide for ChipFill alone, [AggreFill](/products/aggrefill) fills the void. It is a pre-coated aggregate, poured cold, and the repair is finished with the torch and ChipFill, which bonds the aggregate and seals the repair flush. Like ChipFill on its own, it goes in year-round, and the lane reopens within minutes.

Fast Patch DPR is the third: a pourable polymer blend for potholes, spalls, joints, wheel paths and utility cuts, mixed, poured and finished with minimal site preparation. Its page gives the return to service as about an hour at 21°C. In cooler conditions the optional Fast Patch Kicker accelerator shortens the cure, so the work can go on year-round. It bonds to the surrounding pavement and is freeze-thaw resistant, and because it is odourless and 100% solids, it also goes indoors, on warehouse floors.

## Before the first freeze

Walk the lot or the street once, and match each mark to its product:

- Every crack and open joint, and each small pothole: ChipFill.
- Each pothole too deep or wide for ChipFill alone: AggreFill, finished with ChipFill.
- Spalled concrete edges, wheel paths and utility cuts: Fast Patch DPR.
- The closures, from the product pages: minutes for ChipFill and AggreFill, and about an hour at 21°C for Fast Patch DPR, with the Kicker accelerator when it is cooler.
