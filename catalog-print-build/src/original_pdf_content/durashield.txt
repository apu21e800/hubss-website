WHAT IT IS
A two-component waterborne epoxy-modified acrylic pavement maintenance coating, spray + back-roll applied to asphalt. Used for parking lots, residential driveways, and pedestrian areas where colour, slip resistance, and chemical protection are required — but the heavy decorative finish of StreetBond150 isn't needed.

Available in two documented colour variants: Asphalt (black) and Solar Gray. Solar Gray provides solar reflectance for heat-mitigation projects.

KEY FEATURES
- Same flexible epoxy-modified acrylic chemistry as StreetBond — moves with the pavement, will not peel.
- Solar Gray variant: Solar Reflectance = 0.33, contributes to heat-island mitigation.
- VOC < 50 g/L — well below SCAQMD Rule 1113 thresholds.
- Excellent chemical resistance (manufacturer claim) + highly resistant to UV damage.
- Available with anti-slip aggregate broadcast for pedestrian areas.
- 24-month shelf life unopened (40°F–90°F).

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- Type: Two-component waterborne epoxy-modified acrylic
- Density: 14.3 lbs/gal (1.7 kg/L) per ASTM D1475
- Volume solids: 57% (±2) per ASTM D2697
- Weight solids: 75% (±2) per ASTM D2369
- Shore hardness: 80.8 (ASTM D2240)
- Mandrel bend: 1-1/2 in @ 23°C per ASTM D522-93 A
- Water absorption: < 6% (ASTM D570)
- VOC: < 50 g/L (calculated)
- Solar Reflectance (Solar Gray variant): SR = 0.33 initial
- Coverage: 65 sq ft/gallon (1.6 m²/L), 25 mil wet / 14 mil dry, in 2 layers
- Taber abrasion — dry (H-10 wheel): 0.7 g per 1,000 cycles per ASTM D4060
- Taber abrasion — wet (H-10 wheel): 3 g per 1,000 cycles
- Friction — dry: > 65 BPN per ASTM E303
- Friction — wet: > 35 BPN per ASTM E303
- Drying — touch dry: 35 min at 73°F (23°C), 37% RH (ASTM D5895)
- Minimum install temperature: 50°F (10°C) and rising; not to drop below 50°F within 24 hours
- Substrate: Asphalt

COLOURS
Asphalt (black) and Solar Gray. Per TDS, colour "cannot be altered using supplemental pigments."

APPLICATIONS
- Parking lots — asphalt restoration and protection
- Residential driveways — paving and asphalt maintenance
- Pathways, raised medians, pedestrian plazas
- Pedestrian areas (anti-slip aggregate broadcast option)
- Asphalt surfaces exposed to chemicals (fuel, oil, deicers)
- Heat-mitigation surfaces (Solar Gray variant)
