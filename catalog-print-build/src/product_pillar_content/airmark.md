IMPORTANT: AirMark is NON-RUNWAY. Three distinct products live in the AirMark folder: AirMark (taxiways/ramps), PreMark for Airports (landside), WB Airfield Paint (runway-capable).

WHAT IT IS
AirMark is the preformed thermoplastic pavement marking system specifically engineered for airfield use — taxiways, ramps, aprons, gates, and vehicular airside roads. The 'airside' descriptor is what distinguishes AirMark from airport landside markings.

KEY FEATURES
- Preformed thermoplastic with retroreflective glass beads embedded throughout the cross-section.
- Designed to meet FAA airfield marking requirements; references FAA Advisory Circular guidelines and ICAO standards.
- Heat-applied, withstands jet blast, snow clearing, rubber removal treatments, and the daily operational demands of an active airfield.
- 5 standard colours (NOT just white and yellow): Red, White, Yellow, Black, Pink.
- Applied by certified crews — installation efficiency reduces runway / taxiway closure time vs. hand-painted markings.
- Manufactured in an ISO 9001:2015 certified facility.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- Application: Taxiways, ramps, aprons, gates, vehicular airside roads — NON-RUNWAY
- Material: Preformed thermoplastic with retroreflective glass beads embedded throughout cross-section
- Colours: 5 standard — Red, White, Yellow, Black, Pink
- Compliance reference: FAA Advisory Circular (AC 150/5340-1 series) and ICAO standards
- Manufacturing: ISO 9001:2015 certified
- Installation: Heat application by certified crews

APPLICATIONS
- Taxiway centrelines, hold-short markings, designators
- Apron and ramp markings
- Helipad markings
- Gate and stand markings
- Vehicular airside roadway markings
- Ground-vehicle parking and traffic-flow markings
