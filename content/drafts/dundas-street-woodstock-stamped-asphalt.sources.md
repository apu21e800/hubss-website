## How the pattern goes into the street

- "Page 20 of the book sets out StreetPrint's method. Infrared heaters warm existing asphalt until it can be worked and a flexible template presses the pattern in while it is hot; StreetBond pavement coating then colours and seals the surface.": p.20, steps 01 to 03 (lib/idea-book-edition.ts:188–190: "Infrared heaters bring the existing asphalt back to a workable temperature", "Flexible templates stamp the selected pattern ... into the heated surface", "StreetBond creates the colour, and seals the asphalt"), in other words. Step 03's "the industry's most advanced asphalt coating" is a superlative with no source and is left out.
