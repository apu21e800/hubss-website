WHAT IT IS
A custom preformed thermoplastic system for horizontal surface graphics — logos, wayfinding, civic art, school spirit markings, brand identification, community-pride installations. Factory-fabricated to artwork specifications, then heat-fused to asphalt or concrete.

DecoMark is non-reflective and is positioned as branding/identification + directional surface signage — distinct from the regulatory pavement-marking line.

KEY FEATURES
- Made-to-order from vector artwork (CAD or vector files accepted).
- Factory-fabricated multi-colour segments — 24 in × 36 in.
- Heat-fused to asphalt AND concrete (including green concrete).
- Surface signage that lasts 6 to 8 times longer than painted alternatives.
- ADA-compliant pedestrian and wheelchair-friendly surface.
- Manufactured in an ISO 9001:2015 certified facility.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- Material: Preformed thermoplastic — ester-modified rosin, aggregates, pigments, binders, anti-skid elements
- Minimum thickness: 125 mil (3.18 mm)
- Standard sheet size: 24 in × 36 in (0.6 m × 0.91 m), factory-assembled multi-colour segments
- Anti-skid (intermixed): Min 30% intermix; min 9 Mohs hardness (highest in the line)
- Skid resistance: Min 60 BPN per ASTM E303
- Static friction: Min 0.6 (wet & dry) per ASTM C1028 / ASTM D2047
- Minimum install temperature: 45°F (7°C) ambient and road
- Substrate: Asphalt AND Portland cement concrete (including green concrete)
- Installation tools: Propane torch, infrared heater, or blue-flame radiant heater
- Sealer: Two-component sealer required prior to application

PALETTE
