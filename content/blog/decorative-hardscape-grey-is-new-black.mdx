## TrafficPatterns: Subtle Yet Effective for Urban Hardscapes

**TrafficPatterns** is a preformed thermoplastic system designed for crosswalks, pedestrian zones, driveways, and branding applications. Its primary advantages include:

- **High Visibility Without Overstimulation**: White and grey elements create a refined, modern look that still stands out for safety purposes.
- **Fast Installation**: The system is preformed, allowing for rapid implementation with minimal disruption.
- **Slip Resistance**: A textured surface ensures pedestrian safety, even in wet conditions.
- **Low Maintenance**: Highly durable and fade-resistant, reducing upkeep costs.

This makes **TrafficPatterns** an ideal choice for city planners looking to enhance pedestrian and vehicular safety while maintaining a sophisticated, modern streetscape.

## Extreme Durability with TrafficPatternsXD for Complete Streets & Parking Areas

For areas with heavy vehicular traffic, **TrafficPatternsXD** offers an ultra-durable solution. This high-performance thermoplastic is engineered for intersections, arterial road crossings, driveways, and parking lots. Key benefits include:

- **Subtle but Effective Contrast**: Grey tones paired with white create a visually distinct yet aesthetically cohesive design.
- **Extreme Durability**: Thicker thermoplastic construction withstands vehicular impact and harsh weather.
- **Long-Lasting Color**: UV-resistant pigments maintain their vibrancy without excessive brightness.
- **Superior Adhesion**: Fuses with the asphalt surface, preventing peeling or cracking.
- **Cost-Effective**: Reduces the need for frequent repainting or repairs.

Municipalities and commercial property developers rely on **TrafficPatternsXD** to create enduring, visually appealing driveways, crosswalks, and parking spaces.

## StreetBond: The Best Coating Solution for Multi-Use Hardscapes

For projects requiring both decorative and functional enhancements, **StreetBond** is the go-to solution. This durable, flexible coating is perfect for plazas, bike lanes, parking lots, and streetscapes. Key benefits:

- **Customizable Neutrals**: Available in a range of white and grey hues to match urban design themes.
- **Flexible and Crack-Resistant**: Adapts to asphalt and concrete surfaces, reducing surface fatigue and wear.
- **UV-Stable and Environmentally Friendly**: Minimizes heat absorption and prevents color degradation.
- **Skid-Resistant**: Ensures pedestrian safety without compromising aesthetics.

Architects, urban designers, and municipalities use **StreetBond** to create distinctive public spaces that embrace the elegance of neutral tones.
