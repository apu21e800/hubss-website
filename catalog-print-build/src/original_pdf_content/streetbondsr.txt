WHAT IT IS
Solar-reflective version of StreetBond150 — the standard waterborne epoxy-modified acrylic coating is paired with high-SRI pigment colourants to reduce surface temperature and mitigate urban heat island effects.

Engineered specifically for projects targeting LEED v4 Sustainable Sites Credit 7.1 (Heat Island Effect — Non-Roof), climate action plan implementation, or municipal heat-mitigation programs.

KEY FEATURES
- Solar Reflectance Index (SRI) ≥ 29 qualifies for LEED credit — multiple StreetBondSR colours documented above this threshold.
- 'Invisible Shade' colourant technology pushes Solar Reflectance > 0.334 — meeting LEED thresholds without restricting designs to white or light grey only.
- Same flexible adhesion + slip-resistance characteristics as StreetBond150.
- Documented compliance with LEED v4 SSc7.1 (Heat Island Effect) and Green Neighborhood Development GIB Credit 9.
- Manufactured in ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 facilities.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- Base coating: StreetBond150 (waterborne epoxy-modified acrylic)
- Volume solids: 58.2% (ASTM D2697)
- Weight solids: 74.9% (ASTM D2369)
- Density: 13.8 lbs/gal (1.58 kg/L) per ASTM D1475
- VOC: ~19 g/L measured (ASTM D3960-05) — well below SCAQMD Rule 1113
- Friction — wet: 77 BPN (ASTM E303)
- Friction — dry: 81 BPN (ASTM E303)
- Solar Reflectance Index: Limestone Grey SRI = 65; Slate SRI = 33
- Solar Reflectance: ≥ 0.334 with Invisible Shade colourants
- LEED credit: SSc7.1 Heat Island Effect (Non-Roof) — 1 credit at SRI ≥ 29 on ≥ 50% of hardscape
- Flexibility: Passes 6.35 mm @ 21°C; passes 101.6 mm @ -18°C

DOCUMENTED SR-QUALIFYING COLOURS
Marigold 0.35, Avocado 0.35, Pumpkin Spice 0.33, Chestnut Brown 0.34, Limestone Grey SRI 65, Slate SRI 33.

APPLICATIONS
- Parking lots targeting LEED Heat Island Reduction credit
- Civic plazas and pedestrian-priority areas in climate-action zones
- School and campus hardscape (children's surface temperature exposure)
- Urban parks and pathways in heat-vulnerable communities
- Commercial sites with sustainability reporting requirements
