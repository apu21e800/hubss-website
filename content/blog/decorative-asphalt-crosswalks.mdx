## Hardscape Solutions for Decorative Asphalt Crosswalks & More

Safety remains paramount, but crosswalks and decorative hardscapes can also be colourful and fun!

The increasing popularity of colourful, and artfully designed decorative asphalt crosswalks and hardscapes, is exercising the creativity of municipalities, BIA's and commercial developers across Canada.

HUB products for decorative asphalt or concrete crosswalks and all ranges of architectural hardscape treatments not only look appealing, they out perform longer than other decorative paving methods and also add to the safety of pedestrians and drivers.

HUB Surface Systems provides decorative paving products including stamped asphalt, pavement coatings, and preformed thermoplastic treatments. The result is a durable, decorative hardscape surface that achieves a wide range of solutions at a lower cost with less maintenance issues than most alternatives.

### TrafficPatternsXD — Premium Enhanced Stamped Asphalt

TrafficPatternsXD is used all over Canada as a decorative stamped asphalt surface treatment for decorative asphalt crosswalks, hardscapes, streetscapes, and pedestrian areas.

With TrafficPatternsXD you can create designs using virtually any pattern or color that you desire. The preformed thermoplastic treatment is stamped and heat fused securely to the asphalt surface. TrafficPatternsXD stands out as the premium product due to its highly durable qualities and bold looks.

TrafficPatternsXD is low maintenance, holds up in extreme weather conditions, and is the most robust and durable decorative paving product — perfect for roadways and commercial parking lot applications.

### TrafficPatterns — Enhanced Asphalt Surface System

Similar to the XD line, TrafficPatterns can be applied to both asphalt and concrete hardscapes, and is typically used for streetscape designs. This product is for projects that require detailed artistic and architectural designs without the stamping. TrafficPatterns provides custom design flexible options including logos and other illustrative elements. It is heat fused to asphalt or concrete, resulting in long lasting applications that are attractive and durable.

### StreetPrint & StreetBond — Enhanced Stamped Asphalt

The combination of StreetPrint and StreetBond is the most cost-effective option, resulting in attractive, long lasting, decorative paving treatments. The application process involves heating asphalt and stamping the desired pattern using specialized templates, followed by the application of StreetBond, a pavement coating system that bonds to both stamped asphalt and stamped concrete surfaces. StreetBond is available in a wide range of standard and custom colours used to create bold, aesthetically pleasing decorative surfaces. StreetBond also extends the life of the asphalt pavement surface by protecting it from UV and water damage.

### DuraTherm — Enhanced Inset Thermoplastic in Stamped Asphalt

DuraTherm is an imprinted, decorative asphalt paving system made from preformed thermoplastic material that is set into the pavement surface. The process of inlaying DuraTherm by way of asphalt stamping means the material receives zero high-point wear, ensuring that inset designs last for many years. DuraTherm is an excellent choice for decorative asphalt crosswalks, streetscapes, commercial applications, and parking lots.
