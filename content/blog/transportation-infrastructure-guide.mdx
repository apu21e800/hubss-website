# Transportation Infrastructure Guide

## White Papers & Case Studies: Transit and Complete Streets Infrastructure

As Canadian cities accelerate investments in **Complete Streets**, **Vision Zero**, and **climate-resilient mobility networks**, the importance of durable, low-maintenance surface systems has never been clearer.

At **HUB Surface Systems**, we help cities build streets that last — delivering engineered coatings and thermoplastics that improve safety, reduce long-term maintenance costs, and elevate public realm design.

The resources below include **technical white papers** and **real-world case studies** created for **Public Works Directors, Transportation Engineers, Urban Designers, and Landscape Architects**. Whether you're planning a transit corridor, upgrading pedestrian crossings, or specifying materials for a multimodal street, this portal provides the insights you need to build confidently.

## Case Studies

### Extending Transit Lane Lifespan & Reducing Costs Across Canada

**Featured Systems:** MMAX™ (TTC Bus Priority Corridors), TrafficPatternsXD™ (VIVA Next BRT & Brighouse Station)

To boost safety and efficiency along its busiest transit routes, Toronto applied durable MMAX™ surfacing across key TTC bus-priority corridors. The high-performance treatment reinforced lane visibility, reduced maintenance demands, and supported the city's commitment to reliable, long-lasting transit infrastructure.

### Enhancing Safety & Durability in High-Traffic Transit Stations – Waterloo & York, ON

**Featured Systems:** GrandLinq LRT / VIVA Rapidway

York Region enhanced the Highway 7 Rapidway corridor by applying **TrafficPatternsXD™** at intersections and boarding zones, paired with **Green MMAX™** for cycling lanes. This combination improved safety, reduced maintenance, and created a cohesive design standard. The project directly supported the region's **Complete Streets framework**, ensuring long-lasting, safer, and more accessible public spaces.

### Dedicated Bus Lanes and Safe Pedestrian Crossings – East London Link

**Product Integration:** Red MMAX™ for bus lanes and TrafficPatternsXD™ for pedestrian crossings showcased how cities can advance their active transportation goals while maintaining long-term durability. These solutions improved safety and visibility across high-traffic corridors, all without causing major disruptions to transit service during installation.

### Enhancing Multimodal Connectivity – York Region

**Technology in Action:** Leveraging **StreetBond150™** and **TrafficPatternsXD™**, this project showcases the seamless integration of cycling infrastructure with transit lanes and pedestrian crossings to deliver a safer, more accessible corridor. By uniting multiple modes of transportation within a cohesive design, it enhances safety, improves wayfinding, and supports the long-term vision for complete, connected streets in one of Ontario's busiest urban areas.

### Branded Crosswalks & Pedestrian Safety – Vancouver & Richmond

Discover how HUB's customizable thermoplastic systems delivered durable, skid-resistant surfaces that enhance safety and longevity, while also celebrating local cultural identity. From decorative crosswalks in Little Italy on Commercial Drive to custom thermoplastic logos at Brighouse Station, these civic projects showcase how function and design can come together to create vibrant public spaces.
