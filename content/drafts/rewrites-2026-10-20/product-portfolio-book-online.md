Volume 5 of the Product Portfolio Book, the 2026–27 edition, is out. It is HUB Surface Systems' printed book of its ten systems and the jobs that show them in service across Canada, and its cover is the Musqueam crosswalk at the HUB sign at UBC.

## Inside the book

The systems come first, each with its own page of specifications and the uses it is made for: [TrafficPatterns](/products/traffic-patterns), [TrafficPatternsXD](/products/traffic-patterns-xd), [StreetPrint](/products/streetprint), [StreetBond](/products/streetbond), [StreetBondSR](/products/streetbondsr), [DecoMark](/products/decomark), [MMAX](/products/mmax), [DuraTherm](/products/duratherm), [DuraShield](/products/durashield) and [PreMark](/products/premark). The colour charts sit with the systems that have them, and so do the installation steps for TrafficPatternsXD and StreetPrint, with StreetPrint's stamping templates.

Then the applications, each spread with the systems the book specifies for it and the reason for each. Crosswalks, bike lanes, transit corridors and traffic calming are there, and so are play surfaces, splash pads, townhomes, driveways and parking lots.

The then-and-now pages show the same surface years apart: a TrafficPatternsXD crosswalk at a grocery store in Oakville, Ontario, in its first year and its tenth, and a StreetBondSR park path in Osoyoos, British Columbia, when it went in and three years on.

The projects cover the country. Among them are the 14-year-old entrance feature at Toronto Premium Outlets in Halton Hills, Ontario; London, Ontario's transit corridor in TrafficPatternsXD and red MMAX; the white StreetBondSR surface at the Mont-Mégantic observatory in Québec; the cruise ship terminal walkway in Charlottetown, Prince Edward Island; and the crosswalks at the Kwanlin Dün Cultural Centre in Whitehorse, Yukon. Between them are the certified installers who lay the systems, province by province.

The closing pages carry HUB's own numbers, 27 years and 1,000+ projects, coast to coast, and some of the communities HUB works with, the City of Toronto and the University of British Columbia among them. The last spread is the Lunch & Learn: an hour on the systems for your team, in person across Canada or virtual, with lunch on HUB.
