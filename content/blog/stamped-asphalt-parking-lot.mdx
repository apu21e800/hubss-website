## Durable Crosswalks & Traffic Calming Devices That Exceed Performance Expectations

TrafficPatternsXD durable crosswalks and traffic calming devices incorporate a specialized preformed thermoplastic material manufactured by Ennis Flint — North America's largest supplier of roadway markings and paint. TrafficPatternsXD stamped asphalt parking lots incorporate a unique aggregate-reinforced formula with unprecedented wear resistance. The result is a traffic-tough decorative asphalt pavement surface that provides a brick or paving stone aesthetic without the maintenance issues.
