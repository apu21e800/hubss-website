WHAT IT IS
An imprinted, aggregate-reinforced preformed thermoplastic pavement marking system. Two-foot-square sheets are laid on prepared asphalt, heated, and stamped with a wire-rope template — producing a brick-style decorative crosswalk or traffic-calming surface that is permanently bonded to the asphalt below.

The 'XD' stands for 'Extremely Durable.' TrafficPatternsXD is the heavy-duty variant of the TrafficPatterns family, engineered for the most demanding pedestrian and vehicular conditions — bus lanes, BRT corridors, high-volume intersections.

KEY FEATURES
- 150 mil (3.8 mm) minimum thickness — heaviest in the preformed thermoplastic line.
- Aggregate-reinforced through the full cross-section; new anti-skid elements are exposed as the surface wears.
- Sheets stamped into the asphalt with a wire-rope template after heating — produces a brick-style imprinted finish.
- Heat-fused to asphalt — permanent chemical bond, not a coating that can peel.
- Applies at ambient and road temperatures down to 45°F (7°C); no pavement preheating to a specific temperature required.
- Available in 25+ standard colours plus a premium palette (PMS values published in the Colour Guide).

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- Material: Preformed thermoplastic — ester-modified rosin, aggregates, pigments, binders, anti-skid elements
- Minimum thickness: 150 mil (3.8 mm)
- Sheet size: 2 ft × 2 ft (0.61 m × 0.61 m), ±3 mm tolerance
- Anti-skid (intermixed): Minimum 30% intermix; minimum 6 Mohs hardness
- Skid resistance: Minimum 60 BPN per ASTM E303
- Static friction: Minimum 0.6 (wet & dry) per ASTM C1028 / ASTM D2047
- Minimum install temperature: 45°F (7°C) ambient and road
- Substrate: Asphalt only — explicitly NOT for Portland cement concrete
- Retroreflectivity: Non-reflective. Companion white transverse stripe (90 / 125 mil) carries glass beads if required.
- Sealer: Two-part epoxy required prior to application — no VOC solvents; gel time ≥ 20 min at 27°C
- Carton: Max 25 in × 25 in; max 70 lb (32 kg)

APPLICATIONS
- High-volume decorative crosswalks
- BRT corridors and bus priority lanes
- Major intersections subject to heavy turning movements and concentrated wheel loads
- Traffic-calming installations on collector and arterial streets
- Civic plazas with vehicular access through the area
- Through-areas in parking lots
