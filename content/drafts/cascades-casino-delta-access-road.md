A blue and grey sign beside the access road reads “Cascades Casino” and points the way to the casino drop-off, the hotel drop-off, hotel parking, the parkade and deliveries. The road serves Cascades Casino Delta's new hotel in the City of Delta, British Columbia. In the photograph the hotel is still under construction. Across the road runs a ladder crosswalk in [PreMark](/products/premark), HUB Surface Systems' preformed thermoplastic for road markings.

The same photograph opens the PreMark pages of the Product Portfolio Book · Volume 5, on [page 38](/product-book/38), with the caption “City of Delta, British Columbia”.

## Ladder lines cut to specification

The crosswalk is two white edge lines with white bars set between them, the pattern known as a ladder. Ladder lines are on PreMark's list in the book, with arrows, stop bars, yield triangles and bike symbols, and each is cut to the specified shape before it reaches the road. On site the pieces are heated on the asphalt with a torch until they bond to it.

## What goes into the white bars

The book's thickness for PreMark is 125 mil as standard, about 3 mm, with a 90 mil ViziGrip option. The material carries glass beads in it, so the white bars return a driver's headlights after dark. The service life the book gives is 6–8 years. No stencil is needed and there is no cure to wait for: in the book's words, traffic can “drive on immediately”.
