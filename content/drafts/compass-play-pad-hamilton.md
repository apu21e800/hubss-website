On [the play surfaces spread](/product-book/58) of the Product Portfolio Book · Volume 5, one line sits under the headline: "Make concrete and asphalt your canvas." Opposite it is the photograph of a concrete play pad in Hamilton, Ontario, with a compass laid out across it in [StreetBond](/products/streetbond) blue. The caption gives the city and not the park.

## A compass across the whole pad

In the book's photograph the compass runs edge to edge. A pale circle sits at the centre, split by a grey band and ringed in grey, and grey bands run out from the ring to the borders of the pad, so the blue reads as panels set between the lines. Temporary fence panels stand around the pad, and through the fence on the right is the water of the outdoor pool.

The spread lists [DecoMark](/products/decomark) for "custom graphics and play markings", and the site's [playground page](/applications/playgrounds) counts compass roses among the games DecoMark makes, factory-cut in preformed thermoplastic and heat-applied to the surface. In Hamilton the compass is laid out in the coating itself.

## StreetBond on a concrete pad

StreetBond is a water-based, epoxy-modified acrylic pavement coating. It goes on asphalt and concrete. On the play surfaces spread it is the first system specified, with the note "any colour, UV stable". On the playground page, that colour goes down with a slip-resistant texture.

HUB Surface Systems' book gives StreetBond a service life of 8+ years. When the colour wears, the site's playground page says, a play surface is recoated over a summer break rather than rebuilt.
