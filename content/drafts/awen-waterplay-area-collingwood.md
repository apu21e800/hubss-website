A girl stands in a jet of water at the centre of a lime-green circle while a boy runs barefoot across the light-blue ring around it. They are on the Awen' Waterplay Area at Harbourview Park in Collingwood, Ontario, and the colour under their feet is [StreetBond](/products/streetbond) pavement coating. The waterplay area opened in 2023.

HUB Surface Systems prints the same photograph on page 84 of the Product Portfolio Book · Volume 5. Behind the two children, bands of a deeper blue and a green follow the curved edges of the planting beds, and more jets are running where other children play further back.

## Built for feet

Under the photograph the book prints "Preserving and protecting surfaces. Built for feet." On a waterplay area the feet are bare, and the surface is wet whenever the jets are on. The book's spread on splash pads asks for "a slip-resistant texture built for constant water, chemical treatment and bare feet", and StreetBond is the first system it specifies. The site's [splash pads page](/applications/splash-pads) covers how the coating goes onto a pad. StreetBond is a two-part, water-based, epoxy-modified acrylic pavement coating for asphalt and concrete.

## The chemistry under the colour

The facing page, page 85, is headed "The chemistry under the colour" and names the maker: "StreetBond by GAF. The coating chemistry under every StreetBond and StreetPrint surface in this book." The book gives StreetBond a service life of 8+ years.
