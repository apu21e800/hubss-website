Manufacturer: Geveko Markings (Denmark / Sweden). Distributed in Canada by HUB Surface Systems. NOT a HUB-manufactured product.

WHAT IT IS
A hot-applied surface defect repair material designed for permanent restoration of road damage — potholes, cracks, joints, surface defects, cobblestone joints. Pre-formed in chip form, laid into the prepared defect, and activated with a propane heat torch.

All-year-round application capability. Sets rapidly so traffic can re-open within minutes of installation. Used in combination with AggreFill for larger potholes (up to ~1 m²).

KEY FEATURES
- Hot-applied — propane heat torch activation, no specialized equipment or hot-mix plant required.
- Year-round, all-conditions deployment — works through Canadian winters when hot-mix asphalt plants are closed.
- Conforms to defect contour and bonds chemically to surrounding asphalt or concrete.
- Seals the repaired area against water ingress — addresses the freeze-thaw mechanism that turns minor defects into structural failures.
- Sets in minutes — lane can re-open to traffic same shift.
- Pre-formed chip format makes it possible to repair small defects without aggregate batching.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- Type: Hot-applied preformed surface defect repair material
- Application method: Lay into defect, activate with propane heat torch
- Substrate: Asphalt and concrete
- Weather window: All year, all conditions
- Cure / re-open to traffic: Minutes after install
- Defect size — standalone: Smaller defects (cracks, joints, surface defects, small potholes)
- Defect size — with AggreFill: Up to ~1 m² in diameter
- Packaging (per ATS Traffic Canada listing): 26 lb bag, Black

COLOURS
Standard: Black (asphalt match).

APPLICATIONS
- Routine patrol patching (smaller potholes, cracks)
- Emergency response programs
- Utility-cut restoration (small-scale)
- Edge-joint repair
- Cobblestone joint filling
- Sidewalk, pathway, and pedestrian-infrastructure spot repair
- Parking lots, residential driveways, and townhouse pavements
