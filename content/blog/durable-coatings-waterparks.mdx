## Waterproof Coatings

Over the last 5 years, StreetBond Durable Pavement Coatings have been the product of choice for bold, colorful waterpark splash pads design. Slip Resistant, Durable, and available in an almost unlimited range of UV Stable Colours, StreetBond Pavement Coatings are changing waterpark splash pads design by removing limits to your design potential.

With the ever-increasing demand for interesting and creative design within the public space sector, it comes as no surprise that StreetBond Pavement Coatings are leading the way in affordable and durable surface treatment for creating colourful and imaginative waterpark splash pads and other park hardscape features.

Historically, water play parks have been limited to very subtle design elements on their play surfaces. Working with coloured concrete, stains and paints are very limited in this environment. The introduction of StreetBond has expanded its design potential. StreetBond Pavement Coatings offer almost endless creative potential as the substrate becomes the designers' canvas. Bold colours, interesting shapes, and stimulating themes can all be incorporated into the design element.

With over 25 years of history, StreetBond leads the industry with its experience in Canadian climates as well as around the world. StreetBond's proven performance on asphalt and concrete hardscape surfaces mean years of reliable performance.

Manufactured by GAF, high performance StreetBond150 advanced coatings for asphalt and concrete are specially formulated. On one hand, they are flexible enough to move with the pavement surface, so will not crack. On the other hand they are hard enough to withstand heavy wear; too hard they risk cracking; too flexible, they risk prematurely wearing; too smooth being dangerously slippery. StreetBond Pavement Coatings are formulated with balanced performance characteristics in mind.
