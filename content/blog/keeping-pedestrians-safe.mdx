# Keeping Pedestrians Safe and Operation Budgets Low

## Canadian Municipalities Are Proving That Surface Treatments Reduce Crashes—Without Full Reconstruction

As the snow clears, the recurring headache for operations staff returns: faded markings and snowplow damage. In Canada, the "paint-and-patch" cycle isn't just a maintenance task—it's a significant budgetary drain.

**TrafficPatternsXD™** is engineered to break that cycle. By thermally bonding an aggregate-reinforced thermoplastic onto the surface, it provides a solution that is virtually flush with the road, making it resilient to the one thing that destroys everything else: snowplows.

## Three Key Advantages

**Plow-Resistant Durability**

Virtually flush with the asphalt to withstand snowplows, de-icing chemicals, and extreme temperatures. The aggregate-reinforced structure eliminates the raised edges that standard thermoplastic and paint markings develop over time—the very edges that snowplow blades catch and destroy.

**High-Safety Performance**

Provides a slip-resistant, UV-stable surface with high colour contrast for superior pedestrian visibility and accessibility standards compliance. Retroreflectivity is maintained throughout the product's service life, not just at installation.

**Long-Term Budget Savings**

Offers an 8+ year service life with a one-time application, replacing the costly yearly "paint-and-patch" maintenance cycle. Over an 8-year period, municipalities consistently report total cost savings of 40–60% compared to annual repainting programs.

## Field-Proven Across Canada

**City of Vaughan — The Heavy-Duty Standard**

Using TrafficPatternsXD, Vaughan implemented crosswalks that handle high-volume vehicular traffic and aggressive winter maintenance. Because the material is virtually flush with the asphalt, it remains structurally intact during winter snow-clearing operations and maintains high visibility to support pedestrian safety.

**White Rock & Langley, BC — The Architectural Edge**

Utilized TrafficPatterns and TrafficPatternsXD to create a high-end, brick-like aesthetic without the maintenance associated with traditional pavers. The result is a uniform, trip-free surface that enhances the streetscape while maintaining accessibility standards compliance.

**UBC & Commercial Drive — Custom Landmarks**

For the University of British Columbia and Vancouver's "Little Italy" communities, a combination of TrafficPatterns and DecoMark provides custom-branded surface art and wayfinding. These installations have performed flawlessly with a UV-stable and slip-resistant surface through years of heavy vehicular and pedestrian use.
