A basketball court in Montréal, Québec, is coated in two blues and yellow [StreetBond](/products/streetbond), the colour on a surface players stop on and push off from all game long. It is the court on the Sport courts spread of HUB Surface Systems' Product Portfolio Book · Volume 5, opposite the headline "Court colours expanded."

## A court laid out in blocks of colour

[The spread's caption](/product-book/48) names the city and no more; the rest is in the photograph. The court is drawn in fields of colour, and no white lines show on it. A lighter blue covers most of the court, a darker blue marks out the key in front of the near basket, and yellow circles of several sizes sit on both, the largest ringing the post that holds that basket. In places the yellow carries on past the blue, out onto the black asphalt that frames the court. A pale brick building with a row of high windows runs along one side, young shrubs staked against its wall.

## Court with its border and markings in one coating

StreetBond is a pavement coating, and on a court the playing surface, the border and the markings all go down in it, with a texture for grip in the wet. That is the work as the [sport courts page](/applications/sport-courts) describes it, and on this court every shape is a field of the one coating. The same page offers custom colour matching, so a court can carry a school's or a club's own colours.

StreetBond carries a service life of 8+ years in the book, and it is refreshed by recoating. For a court in full sun, the spread pairs it with [StreetBondSR](/products/streetbondsr), its reflective counterpart, noted there as "cooler underfoot".
