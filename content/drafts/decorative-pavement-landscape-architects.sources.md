## Why this piece, and how it differs from the posts on the same ground

- Semrush, Canada, 27 Oct 2026 (keywords-ca.txt): primary "landscape architects" 320 a month, KD 22 (title, slug, excerpt, first paragraph, the H2 "What landscape architects can specify"). (lib/idea-book-edition.ts:151). The piece does not say a landscape architect designed any job except BC Children's, the one job whose records name one.
- Doug's headline is not used as the title or a heading: three short fragments in a row are a machine tell (docs/STYLE.md). The piece keeps his idea in plain words: "the HUB Surface Systems materials that put a design like that on the ground".
- The published posts on this ground are job write-ups or older guides: bc-childrens-hospital-labyrinth, geary-works-toronto-park-walkway and bowen-island-asphalt-path (each linked, a paragraph or two taken from each); streetbondsr-solar-reflective-coatings (Osoyoos, linked); streetbond-leed-urban-heat-island (written for landscape architects too, but on LEED v4, SRI 29 and "up to 17°C", none of which this piece repeats: the book gives LEED v5 and SR 0.33); durable-coatings-waterparks, decorative-paving-solutions and community-identity-surface-design (general StreetBond and DecoMark pieces with no specification table). This piece is the one place that sets the four systems side by side for a designer, by what the design calls for, with the book's specify notes.
- The sibling piece from e-blast 1 (decorative-pavement-product-portfolio-book) also gives TrafficPatterns and StreetBondSR facts; the two use different jobs (Kitchener's veterans crosswalk and Mont-Mégantic there; Kings and Queens, the fleet lot and Osoyoos here) and different words for the same figures. Paint is not mentioned. No count is used as a selling point.
- The e-blast's "8+ year life cycle" for StreetBond is written as service life (docs/GLOSSARY.md:42).
