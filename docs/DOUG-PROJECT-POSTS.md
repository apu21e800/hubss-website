# From job photos to a Projects article

For Doug. Since 19 October 2026 a finished job needs one thing from you: its
photos and a few lines about it, in Studio. 21e8 takes it from there: the
article on hubss.com under **Projects**, and the social posts for it.

## What you do (five minutes, on your phone)

1. Studio (**hubss.com/studio**) → **Job photos for social** → **+**.
2. Add 3 to 10 photos, best one first (it leads the article and the social
   posts), alt text on each: what is in the picture, in a line.
3. **Where**: town and province, spelled out. Add the site if the client is
   happy to be named.
4. **HUB system**, and the **Application** if you like.
5. **What was done**: one or two sentences of facts.
6. **The story**: a few sentences or a list, in your own words: who the
   client was (if they can be named), what they needed, what went in and
   when, who installed it, anything about the design or the colours, and how
   it has held up. Everything the article says about the job comes from here
   and from the line above, so no guesses and no figures you aren't sure of.
   Leave out customers' phone numbers, emails and home addresses. A
   homeowner isn't named in the article, only the town.
   Leave it empty and the photos go to social only, as before.
7. **Publish**.

## What happens next

- 21e8 writes the job up as a Projects article from your words and the
  system's approved copy, nothing else, checks it, and schedules it. It is on
  hubss.com within a few working days; its social posts follow.
- The first photo leads the article; the others close it under "The job in
  photographs", with your captions.
- Once the article exists, the job post shows **The article written from
  this**, so you can get from the photos to the article in one click.
- A live article is edited like any other: change it, press **Publish**, and
  the site updates within seconds.

A job post with a story makes no separate social post of its own: the job goes
out once, as the article, with a link to the full write-up.
