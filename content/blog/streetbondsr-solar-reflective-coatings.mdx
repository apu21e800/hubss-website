# How StreetBondSR Solar Reflective Coatings Make Asphalt Cooler

## A Smarter Solution for Osoyoos' Urban Heat Challenge

As urban areas expand, traditional asphalt surfaces contribute significantly to the **Urban Heat Island (UHI) effect**, raising temperatures and making city environments less comfortable. To combat this, innovative **solar reflective coatings** are changing the way we design streets and pedestrian spaces.

## Osoyoos' Mission: Cooler, More Sustainable Pavement

The town of **Osoyoos, British Columbia**, located in the Okanagan Valley, faced the challenge of **reducing surface temperatures** in its parks and pathways. The goal was to enhance pedestrian comfort while supporting **sustainability efforts**.

## The Solution: StreetBondSR Solar Reflective Coatings

To address this challenge, **StreetBondSR solar reflective coatings** were applied to asphalt surfaces across the town. This cutting-edge technology reflects more solar energy, reducing the heat absorbed by pavement.

## Why StreetBondSR?

- **Heat Reduction:** Lowers asphalt surface temperatures by reflecting solar radiation, reducing the urban heat effect.
- **Enhanced Durability:** Protects asphalt from **UV degradation**, extending its lifespan.
- **Sustainability:** Reducing surface temperatures improves **energy efficiency** in surrounding buildings.
- **Snow Plow Resistant:** Engineered to withstand **harsh Canadian winters** and the impact of snow plows.

## Proven Results in Osoyoos

The integration of **StreetBondSR solar reflective coatings** helped the town achieve:

- **Safer & Cooler Walkways** — Lower temperatures improve pedestrian comfort.
- **Reduced Maintenance Costs** — Extended pavement life means fewer repairs.
- **Better Environmental Impact** — Contributes to overall heat reduction strategies in urban design.

*Installation by Square One Paving Inc.*

## Transforming Urban Spaces with Smarter Asphalt Solutions

Osoyoos' initiative highlights how **StreetBondSR solar reflective coatings** provide an **innovative, sustainable, and cost-effective solution** for modern urban design. By reducing heat absorption and improving surface durability, cities can create **smarter, more livable streetscapes** for residents and visitors alike.
