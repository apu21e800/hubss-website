# Elevating Urban Design with TrafficPatternsXD

Ever heard the phrase "no bricks"? It became a reality when downtown Woodbridge was planning for redevelopment. The project team sought crosswalk solutions that would complement the area's vision while ensuring durability and safety. That's where TrafficPatternsXD urban design solutions, a trusted solution from **HUB Surface Systems Canada**, proved to be the perfect fit.

**Shirley Marsh, Project Manager of Urban Design for the City of Vaughan**, shared insights on the redevelopment:

> *"The Village of Woodbridge is a designated Heritage Conservation District and has long been a hub for social gatherings and celebrations. The district, centered on Woodbridge Avenue, showcases a historic main street setting with many heritage buildings. The Woodbridge streetscape improvement required careful selection of plantings, street furniture, and paving materials. The City chose **TrafficPatternsXD solutions** for all pedestrian crosswalks at intersections to enhance pedestrian zones and improve safety. The project had multiple goals: to beautify the street environment, create safe traffic circulation, enhance the heritage village character, ensure material durability, and enable efficient operations and maintenance. The team feels we successfully achieved these goals in the completion of the Woodbridge Avenue streetscape improvement project."*
