THE INSTALL
Lay. Bond. Stamp. Done.
Three steps, one visit — no pavers, no expansion joints, no trip hazards.
01 Lay. Preformed panels go down on the existing asphalt — in your pattern, in your colours.
02 Bond. Infrared heat melts the material and thermally bonds it into the road itself.
03 Stamp. The pattern is pressed flush into the surface — nothing raised, nothing for a plow blade to catch.
