**Location:** Port Coquitlam, BC, Canada

## A Civic Landmark in Decorative Asphalt

Port Coquitlam's Terry Fox plaza became a wayfinding landmark when StreetBond SB150 coatings and DecoMark custom thermoplastic transformed the civic space into a vibrant, navigable tribute — proof that infrastructure can carry a community's story as well as its foot traffic.

## StreetBond SB150 Advanced Coatings

StreetBond bonds permanently to asphalt and concrete surfaces to provide both a durable and decorative finish that is low-maintenance and protects and extends the life of the pavement. With 25 years of experience in specialized asphalt coating development, StreetBond advanced coatings have been proven in a variety of conditions in over 40 countries worldwide.

## DecoMark — Custom Wayfinding and Horizontal Surface Markings

DecoMark is a custom wayfinding and horizontal surface markings and graphics solution for application to both asphalt and concrete. DecoMark has a wide range of potential applications including wayfinding devices, traffic guidance markings, and park and pathway directional markings. Engineered to last 6 to 8 times longer than paint in areas exposed to traffic and even longer in areas with only pedestrian traffic.
