WHAT IT IS
A high-performance waterborne epoxy-modified acrylic pavement coating that turns ordinary asphalt or concrete into coloured, durable, slip-resistant surfaces — bike lanes, bus priority lanes, decorative crosswalks, plazas, parks, and branded private installations.

StreetBond is a system, not a single product. The line includes water-based variants (SB120, SB150, SB150 AL), MMA-based variants for cold-climate work (Pro 220, Pro 250), and concrete primers (QS solvent-based, WB waterborne).

KEY FEATURES
- Waterborne epoxy-modified acrylic — flexes with the pavement, resists peeling.
- 73+ standard colours plus custom colour matching. 'Invisible Shade' technology pushes Solar Reflectance above 0.334 even on darker colours.
- ADA-compliant.
- VOC < 50 g/L (calculated) for the water-based variants — well below SCAQMD Rule 1113 limits.
- 1-year limited manufacturer warranty (additional coverage available).
- Documented field performance: 10+ years on Beale Street (Memphis); 12+ years in Padua (Italy).

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- Product family: SB120 + SB150 + SB150 AL (water-based); Pro 220 + Pro 250 (MMA / PMMA); Concrete Primer QS + WB
- Chemistry (water-based): Two-component waterborne epoxy-modified acrylic
- Density: SB120 14.0 lb/gal; SB150 13.7 lb/gal; SB150 AL 11.1 lb/gal
- Volume solids: SB120 54–61%; SB150 53.5–60.6%; SB150 AL 45.2 ± 2%
- VOC: < 50 g/L (water-based)
- Drying — touch dry: 1–4 hr at 77°F (25°C), 40% RH (ASTM D5895)
- Cure to traffic: 6–24 hours (climate dependent)
- Application temperature: 50°F (10°C) and rising, up to 113°F (45°C)
- Coverage (3 layers, pedestrian): ~200 ft² per unit (~49 ft²/gal)
- Coverage (4 layers, vehicular): ~150 ft² per unit (~36 ft²/gal)
- Friction — SB150: Dry 55–90 BPN / Wet 35–70 BPN (ASTM E303)
- Adhesion: 300–1,400 psi (ASTM D4541)
- Pro 220/250 application temp: 32°F to 95°F (0–35°C)
