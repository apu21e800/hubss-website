WHAT IT IS
Pre-cut preformed thermoplastic regulatory pavement markings — arrows, stop bars, yield triangles, school-zone legends, bike-lane pictograms, accessible parking symbols, crosswalk lines, rumble strips. Heat-applied with a propane torch, with no surface preheating required. Lasts 6 to 8 times longer than paint.

PreMark is offered in four manufacturing finishes (BD, VG, NB, SK) that determine surface texture and retroreflectivity. PreMarkXF is the cold-weather variant with no minimum road or ambient temperature requirement.

KEY FEATURES
- Heat-applied via industrial propane torch — drive-on immediately after installation.
- Made with up to 60% recycled material.
- Impervious to oil and gasoline.
- Manufacturing finishes: BD (Beaded — full retroreflectivity), VG (ViziGrip — beads + anti-skid), NB (Non-Beaded — for reversible arrows), SK (Skid-Only — for coloured preferential lanes).
- PreMarkXF cold-weather variant: "No minimum road or ambient temperature requirements for application."
- 24-month shelf life (PreMark); 2 years (PreMarkXF).
- Manufactured in ISO 9001:2015 certified facility.
- ADA-compliant pedestrian and wheelchair-friendly surface.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- Material: Preformed thermoplastic — high-quality resin, binder, aggregate; up to 60% recycled material
- Manufacturing finishes: BD (Beaded), VG (ViziGrip), NB (Non-Beaded), SK (Skid-Only)
- Installation: Industrial propane heat torch — no surface preheating required
- Application temperature (PreMarkXF): No minimum road or ambient temperature requirements
- Retroreflectivity (BD / VG): Factory-applied surface beads + intermix glass beads, exposed as surface wears
- Skid resistance: VG and SK variants carry anti-skid elements
- Substrate: Asphalt and concrete (sealer recommended for concrete)
- Shelf life: 24 months (PreMark) / 2 years (PreMarkXF)

COLOURS
10 standard regulatory colours: Black, Blue, Purple, Red, Grey, Green, Orange, White, Bike Lane Green, Yellow.

APPLICATIONS
- Turn arrows, stop bars, yield triangles
- School zone legends and school-area markings
- Bike pictograms and lane symbols
- Accessible parking symbols (ISA — International Symbol of Accessibility)
- Crosswalk ladder lines and continental crosswalk patterns
- Rumble strips and traffic-calming legends
- Coloured preferential lane symbols (SK variant)
- Reversible arrows (NB variant)
