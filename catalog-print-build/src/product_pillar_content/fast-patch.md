WHAT IT IS
FastPatch DPR (Distressed Pavement Repair) is a polymer-blend pourable repair material for asphalt and concrete — engineered for high-strength, fast-return-to-service repair of potholes, spalls, joints, wheel paths, and utility cuts. Mixed with a cordless drill, poured into the defect, and back in service in less than 45 minutes.

Made from a unique polymer blend with up to 80% recycled and renewable materials. Odourless — suitable for indoor environments where ventilation rules out hot-mix or solvent-based materials.

KEY FEATURES
- Back in service in less than 45 minutes (1 hr at 75°F, 2–3 hr at 50–65°F).
- FastPatch Kicker accelerator available for cooler-weather work — shortens cure time further.
- 100% solids, 80% recycled/renewable polymer matrix.
- Completely odourless — suitable for warehouse floors, loading docks, underground parking.
- Excellent freeze-thaw resistance and impact absorption.
- Snowplow-friendly.
- Available in asphalt-repair and concrete-repair formulations.
- Pre-measured kits: 5-gallon (11 L), 3-gallon (5 L), 1-gallon (2.5 L).

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- Type: Polymer-blend distressed pavement repair material
- Composition: Polymer matrix with up to 80% recycled and renewable materials; 100% solids
- Application method: Mix with cordless drill, pour, finish
- Cure time at 75°F (24°C): 1 hour
- Cure time at 50–65°F (10–18°C): 2–3 hours
- Return to service: < 45 minutes (with appropriate temperature/accelerator)
- Cold-weather option: FastPatch Kicker accelerator
- Substrate: Asphalt and concrete (separate formulations available)
- Kit sizes: 5 gal (11 L) / 3 gal (5 L) / 1 gal (2.5 L)
- Odour: Odourless — suitable for indoor application
- Manufacturer: The Willamette Valley Company LLC (USA)

COLOURS
Black (asphalt formulation) and concrete-grey (concrete formulation). Not a decorative product.

APPLICATIONS
- Roadways and bridges — spalls, crack repair, joint repair, broken corners, surface leveling, potholes
- Airports — spalls, wheel-path areas, approaches and departures
- Parking lots — potholes, walkways, broken areas
- Warehouses — floor spalls, loading-dock areas
- Sidewalks and curbs — trip-hazard remediation, walkway repair
- Indoor environments where ventilation excludes solvent/asphalt-emulsion products
