Stamped-asphalt pattern catalogue

Every distinct stamped-asphalt pattern from the source PDFs, vectorized and tileable. Each pattern in four variants: brand orange, neutral white, muted bronze (all soft / background-style), plus a single-line crisp linework variant for elegant minimal applications.

Source PDFs in apshalt patterns/. Brick geometry extracted from Standard Herringbone.pdf (2.08:1 ratio, joint 0.376 in) and applied consistently across all patterns. All patterns are stroke-only (no fills) so opacity, colour, and scale tune freely.
