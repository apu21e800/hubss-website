“Constant water, chemical treatments, and bare feet make waterparks one of the harshest environments in outdoor pavement.” That is the first sentence on [page 129](/product-book/129) of the Product Portfolio Book · Volume 5, printed under a photograph of the spray park at Bear Creek Park in Surrey, British Columbia. The caption names the HUB Surface Systems coating on it: [StreetBond](/products/streetbond).

## Blue and green ribbons on pale concrete

The photograph looks down on the spray park. Ribbons of blue and green wind across the pale concrete, with pale fish drawn in the blue. On the right, a green band carries yellow flowers and leaves. In the middle, a round island of blue ringed in green sits under a large tree. A yellow arch and an orange ball stand among the ribbons, and children and adults are out on the pad.

## The coating on the pad

The book's [splash pads](/applications/splash-pads) spread lists StreetBond as skid resistant and UV stable. The book gives this water-based, epoxy-modified acrylic 8+ years of service and calls it easily refreshed.

Page 129 heads its own text “Waterpark surface coating.” After the sentence about harsh environments, it turns to the coating: “StreetBond builds a slip-resistant texture that meets wet-surface safety expectations while holding vivid colour season after season.”
