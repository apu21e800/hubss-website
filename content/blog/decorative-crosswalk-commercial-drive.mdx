## Community Branding

Three intersections on Vancouver's Commercial Drive became the neighbourhood's most recognizable street-level identity markers in 2019 — Italian flag colours embedded in thermoplastic, installed just in time for Italian Day on The Drive and celebrated by residents, Vancouverites, and tourists ever since.

<div className="my-8 rounded-xl overflow-hidden">
  ![Commercial Drive decorative crosswalk](/images/blog/decorative-crosswalk-commercial-drive/featured.jpg)
</div>

The City of Vancouver project was installed by Square One using a combination of DecoMark custom graphics and TrafficPatterns decorative thermoplastic. DecoMark is a custom wayfinding and horizontal surface markings solution for application to both asphalt and concrete, with a wide range of potential applications including wayfinding devices, traffic guidance markings, and park and pathway directional markings.

TrafficPatterns is a durable preformed thermoplastic pavement markings material used primarily for streetscape and traffic calming purposes on public roads and commercial parking lots. The crosswalks are on East 1st Avenue and Commercial, East 4th Avenue and Commercial, and Charles Street and Commercial. Stretching eight blocks, Little Italy is four blocks of Commercial Drive north and south of East 1st Avenue. The City of Vancouver recognized these eight blocks of Commercial Drive as Vancouver's historic Little Italy, so we wanted to give some definition to the area, and promote and expand upon that, and it's been really well received both by residents, Vancouverites, and tourists that are visiting the city.
