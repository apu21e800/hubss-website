## TrafficPatterns Decorative Crosswalk Application

The traffic pattern crosswalk upgrade for Tsain-Ko Centre is located at 5500 Sunshine Coast Hwy, at the southern entrance to the Town of Sechelt on shishalh Nation territory. Tsain-Ko Centre has expanded to over 100,000 square feet of retail space with over 15 national retailers. The Centre showcases several cultural areas and artworks, and is planning a final expansion of more than 5,000 square feet in 2021-2022.

The crosswalk design is created with the patented TrafficPatterns product line, and contains an aboriginal motif, designed to draw attention to the crosswalk, providing aesthetically attractive road safety functionality.
