v4 · true single-line fix + architectural variants

Stamped-asphalt patterns — single-line corrected

The .line variant in v3 drew each brick as a closed outline, so every joint rendered as two parallel lines (the right edge of brick A + the left edge of brick B). v4 fixes this: each brick body is expanded by half the joint width before edge extraction, so adjacent bricks share their joint-centerline edges. Coincident edges are then deduped and collinear sub-segments merged so every joint is emitted as exactly one continuous line.

Two architectural variants added on top of the true single-line: blueprint (light cyan on deep navy) and draft (dark navy on cream paper). Both use the same deduped geometry, different palettes.

Source PDFs in apshalt patterns/. 8 patterns × 7 variants = 56 SVGs. The 3 new variants (.single-line, .blueprint, .draft) are the focus of this round; the 4 from v3 are kept for comparison.
