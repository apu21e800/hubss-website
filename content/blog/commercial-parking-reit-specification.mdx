# Why Canada's Top REITs Are Specifying Decorative Pavement

Your parking lot is the first thing a tenant sees. The last thing they touch before they leave. And for most commercial properties, it looks exactly like every other parking lot in Canada.

That's a missed opportunity — and Canada's leading REITs have started to notice.

## Parking as Brand Real Estate

Surface systems like StreetBond and TrafficPatterns are being specified on commercial properties not just for durability, but for identity. A well-designed parking surface communicates maintenance standards, tenant quality, and property pride before a single tenant steps inside.

York Region has used HUB systems across dozens of municipal intersections. The City of Toronto specifies our thermoplastic on high-pedestrian corridors. The reason is simple: surfaces that look intentional attract tenants who are intentional.

## The Four Systems Commercial Properties Are Specifying

**StreetBond — Coloured Pavement Coating**
High-performance colour coating for parking lanes, fire routes, accessible spaces, and drive aisles. Bonds directly to existing asphalt — no tear-out required. 20-year durability. Available in custom brand colours.

**TrafficPatterns — Preformed Thermoplastic**
Pre-fabricated thermoplastic panels for parking lot logos, directional arrows, pedestrian crossings, and accessible markings. Install in hours, not days. Zero cure time.

**DecoMark — Decorative Marking System**
Combines high-visibility safety performance with graphic design capability. Used for wayfinding, branding, and durability in a single application.

**DuraShield — Protective Sealer**
Clear protective coating that seals and rejuvenates existing asphalt. Extends pavement life by 5–8 years, reduces lifecycle costs significantly.

## Three Canadian Case Studies

### Fairview Park Mall — Kitchener, Ontario
StreetBond coloured coating across 340,000 sq ft of surface parking. Six years post-installation with zero reapplication required. The property management team reports a measurable reduction in tenant complaints about lot condition.

### Toronto Premium Outlets — Halton Hills, Ontario
Full TrafficPatterns installation across pedestrian crossings and high-traffic drive aisles. Thirteen years of performance in a high-volume retail environment. The system outlasted two conventional paint cycles in the same period.

### Fortinos — Multiple GTA Locations
StreetBond applied to accessible parking zones and fire routes across 14 properties. Ten-year performance with consistent colour retention. Facilities management confirmed a 60% reduction in annual marking maintenance spend.

## The Budget Argument

Traditional painted markings cost $0.08–$0.15/sq ft annually through repainting. StreetBond runs $1.20–$2.50/sq ft installed — once. Over a 10-year window, the math consistently favours decorative surface systems, particularly when you factor in traffic management, contractor mobilization, and tenant disruption during annual repaints.

As Stantec's urban design team noted in a recent specification review: *"The total cost of ownership for thermoplastic and resin-based systems is substantially lower than painted alternatives when assessed over a 10-year horizon."*
