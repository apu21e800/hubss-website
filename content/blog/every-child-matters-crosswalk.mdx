## Every Child Matters Crosswalk Built with TrafficPatterns

The Town of Georgina in York Region turned a municipal crosswalk into a permanent act of remembrance — an Every Child Matters installation using TrafficPatterns thermoplastic that carries the weight of national reconciliation into the daily life of a community intersection.

## Product Specifications

TrafficPatterns is a durable preformed thermoplastic pavement marking material used in hardscape installations for streetscapes and crosswalks. It is engineered with safety, durability, and aesthetics in mind. Surface applied and virtually maintenance-free, TrafficPatterns comes in a variety of colours and patterns and can be manufactured to create custom designs on both asphalt or concrete.
