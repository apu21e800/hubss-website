WHAT IT IS
An interconnected preformed thermoplastic pavement marking system that simulates pavers or stones. Pre-cut interlocking sheets are surface-applied with infrared heat — no stamping templates or grids required.

The standard-duty product in the TrafficPatterns family. Suitable for decorative crosswalks, parks, schools, and public spaces where TPXD's heavier 150 mil cross-section is not required. Works on BOTH asphalt and concrete — a key differentiator from TPXD.

KEY FEATURES
- 125 mil (3.2 mm) minimum thickness — preformed at the factory.
- Interconnected sheet design simulates pavers; no stamping template required.
- Applies to both asphalt AND Portland cement concrete, including 'green' concrete.
- Heat-applied with an infrared heater — heating indicators on the surface signal molten state.
- Anti-skid elements harder than TPXD — minimum 8 Mohs (vs. 6 Mohs for TPXD).
- Open to traffic within minutes of application (manufacturer marketing).

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- Material: Preformed thermoplastic — ester-modified rosin, aggregates, pigments, binders, anti-skid elements
- Minimum thickness: 125 mil (3.18 mm)
- Sheet size: Interconnected 24×24 in. Solid Sheets variant: 24×36 in.
- Anti-skid (intermixed): Min 30% intermix; min 8 Mohs hardness
- Skid resistance: Min 60 BPN per ASTM E303
- Static friction: Min 0.6 (wet & dry) per ASTM C1028 / ASTM D2047
- Minimum install temperature: 45°F (7.2°C) ambient and road
- Substrate: Asphalt AND Portland cement concrete (including green concrete)
- Installation: Infrared heat — no stamping template, no grid

PATTERNS
Running Bond, Herringbone, Plain Weave, Basket Weave, Ashlar, Stacked Tile (large + small), Diagonal Tile (large + small), Honeycomb, Courtyard, Quarter Round, Terrazzo. Standard borders TP36 through TP42.

APPLICATIONS
- Decorative crosswalks (standard duty)
- Parks and pathways
- Schools and playgrounds (regulatory + decorative)
- Public spaces and civic plazas
- Parking lot through-areas and design elements
- Pedestrian-safety treatments where TPXD's heavier 150 mil is not required
