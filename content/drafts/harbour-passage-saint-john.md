Harbour Passage is the waterfront walkway in Saint John, New Brunswick, and where it passes Market Slip, beside Market Square, it is coated in red [StreetBond](/products/streetbond). The photograph of that stretch faces StreetBond's own page in HUB Surface Systems' Product Portfolio Book · Volume 5, captioned with the city and the walkway's name.

## Red along the harbour railing

The book's photograph puts the red right beside the railing at the water's edge, separated from it by a row of dark grey pavers. The railing has a wooden top rail over horizontal cables, and the lamp posts along the walk are crowned with spoked wheels. Ahead, the red's inner edge curves in around a planting bed beside a flight of concrete steps, and blue cranes stand on the far side of the harbour under a low grey sky.

## The page it faces

StreetBond's page, printed beside the photograph on [pages 24 and 25](/product-book/24), is headed "The colour." Its specification runs to four lines: a water-based, epoxy-modified acrylic; asphalt and concrete; colours from the standard range or custom-matched; and a service life of 8+ years, easily refreshed.
