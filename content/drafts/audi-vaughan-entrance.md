[The Product Portfolio Book · Volume 5](/product-book/109) files the Audi dealership in Vaughan, Ontario, under Retail, with the headline “First impressions.” The surface at the entrance is [TrafficPatternsXD](/products/traffic-patterns-xd), aggregate-reinforced preformed thermoplastic from HUB Surface Systems and, in the book's words, “the toughest material in the product range”.

In the book's photograph the pattern runs along the front of a grey building, with the four rings and the red Audi lettering high above its overhead glass doors. It is a grid of light grey squares with a border of darker grey, and where it meets the dark asphalt beside it the edge is a straight line.

The words on the page compare the lines to the brand on the wall, “Like the Audi brand, crisp, clean lines”, and end on what the surface does for the architecture: “Accenting the building in a unique way.”

## How the grid gets its lines

TrafficPatternsXD goes in on asphalt only, in the three steps the book prints as Place, Heat and Stamp. The lines of a grid like this one are the stamping template's impressions, pressed through the material into the top of the asphalt.

Anti-skid aggregate rated 8–9 on the Mohs scale runs through the full cross-section, so the grip lasts as the surface wears. Page 13, the system's own, gives skid resistance as 60 BPN under ASTM E303 and the service life as 10+ years.

## Where a dealership entrance sits in the book

The book's spread on [commercial spaces](/applications/commercial-spaces) names one system, TrafficPatternsXD, for “pedestrian channelization; the look of pavers, stones and cobbles”.
