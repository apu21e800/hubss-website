## The Cost of Looking Cheap

Traditional paint-based parking markings have a service life of 1–2 years in Canadian conditions. Add aggressive winters, heavy plow traffic, de-icing salt, freeze-thaw cycling, and you've got markings that look tired within a single season.

The math is quietly brutal:

- **Year 1:** Fresh paint, professional appearance
- **Year 2:** Touch-ups, re-striping — full cost again
- **Year 3–5:** Full reapplication cycles, slip-and-fall liability exposure, tenant complaints

Over a 10-year property hold, a REIT managing 40+ properties isn't budgeting for paint — they're funding a maintenance programme that never ends and never gets better.

> The real cost of cheap markings isn't the material. It's the labour, the disruption, the liability exposure, and the brand impression delivered to every customer who visits your property.

What does a neglected parking lot communicate? To a retail tenant at renewal, it signals underinvestment. To a customer choosing between your power centre and the competition, it's a friction point. Small — but real.

There's a better model.

## Four Products. Every Parking Surface Scenario.

HUB Surface Systems has engineered four distinct systems for commercial parking, each purpose-built for a specific performance requirement.

### TrafficPatterns® — Preformed Thermoplastic Marking

The baseline standard for commercial parking. Preformed thermoplastic is heat-fused directly into the asphalt surface — not applied on top of it. The result is a marking that is flush-mounted, plow-blade resistant, and chemically bonded to the surface.

- **Service life:** 8–10 years under normal commercial traffic
- **Plow resistance:** Flush with surface — no blade catch point
- **Application:** Single session, trafficable within minutes
- **Best for:** Stall lines, directional arrows, accessible parking, fire routes

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: TrafficPatterns stall markings in commercial parking lot — close-up showing flush, clean surface]

### TrafficPatternsXD™ — Built for High-Volume Punishment

When your property sees relentless daily traffic — grocery-anchored retail, big box, fast food drive-throughs — standard thermoplastic isn't enough. TrafficPatternsXD is 150 mil thick (versus 90 mil standard), aggregate-reinforced for all-weather skid resistance, and purpose-built for surfaces where degradation is rapid.

- **Thickness:** 150 mil — 65% thicker than standard thermoplastic
- **Surface:** Anti-skid aggregate for all-weather grip
- **Service life:** 8+ years in the most demanding commercial environments
- **Best for:** High-volume commercial, drive-through lanes, grocery anchors, fuel bar entries

> "TrafficPatternsXD has proven to be very stable and durable, with virtually no maintenance required since installation. The markings continue to look great." — Leo Guddemi, Stantec

### StreetPrint® — When the Parking Lot Is Part of the Brand

Some properties don't want a parking lot — they want an arrival experience. StreetPrint transforms asphalt into stamped brick, slate, cobblestone, or custom tile patterns using a durable pigmented coating over a stamped surface.

No pavers. No trip hazards. No maintenance nightmare.

- **Patterns:** Brick, slate, cobblestone, herringbone, custom — any pattern, any colour
- **No trip hazards:** Flush with surrounding asphalt, unlike real pavers
- **Durability:** 10–15 years with proper application
- **Best for:** Retail forecourts, entry drives, pedestrian crossings within parking fields, town centre commercial

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: StreetPrint stamped brick entry drive at premium retail development — warm tones, brand-aligned colours]

### StreetBond® / StreetBondSR — Protective Coating + Solar Reflectivity

StreetBond is a polymer-modified coating that seals and colours asphalt — improving surface durability while delivering a highly visible, clean result. StreetBondSR adds Solar Reflectance (SR) technology: the coating reflects heat rather than absorbing it, reducing surface temperatures by up to 10°C and contributing to LEED compliance.

- **LEED contribution:** Reduced urban heat island effect
- **Visibility:** Bright, clean colour over full surface areas — fire routes, accessible zones
- **StreetBondSR:** Qualifies for sustainability programmes and LEED credits
- **Best for:** Fire routes, pedestrian zones, accessible parking areas, LEED-seeking developments

## Built for Canadian Winters

Canadian winters are the ultimate performance test for any pavement marking system. What looks sharp in September can be unrecognizable by April.

HUB systems are engineered specifically for:

- **Temperature range:** -40°C to +40°C service range — no cracking, no delamination
- **Plow resistance:** Flush-mounted, no edge catch point for blade damage
- **De-icing chemicals:** Resistant to chloride-based and acetate-based ice melters
- **Freeze-thaw cycling:** Thermally bonded — expands and contracts with the pavement substrate

| Snow plow resistance | Poor — chips and lifts | Excellent — flush mount | Excellent — flush mount |

| Skid resistance | None | Standard | Aggregate-reinforced |

## The Smart Budget Decision

The conversation usually starts with: *"What's the cost per square foot?"*

The better question: *"What's the cost per square foot, per year, over 10 years?"*

**Traditional paint (annual re-stripe cycle):**
- Material + labour × 10 years
- Property disruption (cones, closures, tenant friction) × 10 years
- Liability exposure during degraded-marking periods
- Management time coordinating annual contractors

**TrafficPatternsXD (one application):**
- Single installation investment
- Zero maintenance budget for 8–10 years
- No disruption after installation day
- No liability exposure from illegible markings

The lifecycle math consistently favours thermoplastic by a significant margin — before accounting for the brand impression difference between a parking lot that looks maintained versus one that looks neglected.

For REIT portfolios managing dozens of properties, the multiplier effect is substantial: one specification standard, trained applicator network, consistent performance across every site.
