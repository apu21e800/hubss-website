**Location:** York Region — Town of Georgina, ON, Canada

## Every Child Matters Crosswalk Built with TrafficPatterns

TrafficPatterns is a durable preformed thermoplastic pavement marking material used in hardscape installations for streetscapes and crosswalks. It is engineered with safety, durability, and aesthetics in mind.

TrafficPatterns products provide a cost-effective alternative to the use of brick pavers because the material is surface applied and virtually maintenance-free. TrafficPatterns comes in a variety of colours and patterns and can be manufactured to create custom designs on both asphalt or concrete.
