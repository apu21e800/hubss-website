People crossing at Commercial and Bastion Streets in downtown Nanaimo, British Columbia, walk over the colours of the Progress Pride flag, which fill the intersection from curb to curb. The intersection is [TrafficPatterns](/products/traffic-patterns) preformed thermoplastic, finished in June 2025, and its photograph has page 126 to itself in HUB Surface Systems' Product Portfolio Book · Volume 5.

## Rainbow bands and a chevron

The view is from high on one corner. Curved bands of red, orange, yellow, green, blue and purple sweep across most of the intersection, and from one side a chevron cuts in, in black, brown, light blue and pink. White lines mark the crosswalks on all four sides, and the colour carries through them to the curbs. Storefronts stand at the corners.

## The book's page for Nanaimo

The page's label is Pride, and its headline is "Community colours." The book's [community branding](/applications/community-branding) spread ends on "The street can tell the story."

## What the colours are made of

TrafficPatterns is cut to the design in the factory, at 125 mil, so what arrives on site is the design itself, ready to be heated onto the road until it bonds. Its page in the book closes on "Custom designs to fit with the community."

The colour is the thermoplastic's own, and the book's thermoplastic colour pages describe the range as "Formulated for UV stability," with physical samples on request. Anti-skid aggregate runs through the material as well, and the book puts the skid resistance at 60 BPN under ASTM E303. An installation opens to traffic within hours of going down, and the book gives TrafficPatterns a service life of 8+ years.
