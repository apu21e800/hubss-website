HUBSS Product Source of Truth — AggreFill
Manufacturer: Geveko Markings (Denmark / Sweden). Distributed in Canada by HUB Surface Systems.
Category: CONCRETE & ASPHALT REPAIR

WHAT IT IS
A pre-coated aggregate filler used in combination with ChipFill to permanently repair larger potholes — up to approximately 1 m² in diameter. AggreFill provides the structural mass to fill the void; ChipFill bonds the aggregate matrix and seals the repaired surface.

The AggreFill + ChipFill system is positioned as a permanent repair, not a seasonal temporary patch. Year-round deployment, traffic-ready within minutes.

KEY FEATURES
- Two-product system: AggreFill fills the void, ChipFill bonds and seals.
- Larger pothole capacity — up to ~1 m² diameter and deeper than ChipFill alone can address.
- Cold-applied aggregate plus heat-torch ChipFill matrix — no hot-mix plant required.
- Year-round, all-conditions deployment.
- Sets in minutes — lane can re-open to traffic same shift.
- Pre-coated aggregate produces a chemical bond to the surrounding pavement when heat-activated alongside ChipFill.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- Type: Pre-coated aggregate filler — paired with Geveko ChipFill
- Application method: Cold-applied aggregate, then heat-torch ChipFill matrix above and around
- Substrate: Asphalt and concrete
- Repair size: Up to approximately 1 m² in diameter
- Cure / re-open to traffic: Minutes after install (matches ChipFill)
- Weather window: All year, all conditions

APPLICATIONS
- Larger road potholes (up to ~1 m²)
- Parking lot craters
- Industrial-site pothole damage
- Utility cut restoration (medium-scale)
- Driveway and townhouse pavement deep repair
- Any pothole too deep or wide for stand-alone ChipFill
