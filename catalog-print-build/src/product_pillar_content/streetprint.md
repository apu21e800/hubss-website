WHAT IT IS
A genuine stamped-asphalt system. The asphalt itself is reheated in place with StreetHeat® infrared equipment, imprinted with a wire-rope template using a vibratory plate compactor, then coated with StreetBond150 for colour and protection.

The finished surface IS the asphalt — there is no separate paver, no thermoplastic inlay, no joints. This makes the surface flush with the surrounding road and structurally compatible with municipal winter maintenance.

KEY FEATURES
- Surface is the asphalt itself — imprinted, not a separate material laid on top.
- Flush with surrounding pavement — no raised edges, no shear risk under snowplow blades.
- More than 50 million square feet installed since 1992.
- 100+ template SKUs across 15 pattern families.
- Colour from StreetBond150 — full 73+ colour StreetBond palette.
- Left uncoated, the imprinted asphalt can be opened to traffic immediately.
- Must be installed by a certified StreetPrint applicator. HUBSS maintains the Canadian certification.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- System: Imprinted asphalt + StreetBond150 colour coat
- Imprint depth: Minimum 3/8 inch — areas less than 3/8" must be reheated and re-stamped
- Asphalt heating depth: Minimum 1/2 inch (12.5 mm) without burning the surface
- Surface temperature limit during heating: Must not exceed 325°F (162°C) by infrared
- Recommended HMA thickness: 1.5–2 inches compacted
- Open to traffic — uncoated: Immediately
- Open to traffic — coated: 6–24 hours per StreetBond150 cure schedule
- Pattern library: 15 families, 100+ individual template SKUs
- Template sizes: SR-120, SR-60, SR-20 (proprietary); paver-compatible options
- Substrate: Asphalt only — not for patios, pool decks, or concrete
