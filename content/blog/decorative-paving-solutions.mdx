# Decorative Paving Solutions for Streets & Public Spaces

## Why Engaging Public Spaces Matter

Public spaces are more than infrastructure — they're the connective tissue of community life. Parks, plazas, sidewalks, and streets shape how people move, gather, and feel in a city. They influence safety, identity, and quality of life.

Decorative paving is a powerful tool in shaping these experiences. When surfaces are designed with intention, they become more than just functional — they become **narrative assets**. Visual texture, vibrant color, and thoughtful placement can turn an intersection into a gateway, a sidewalk into a statement, and a schoolyard into a landmark.

> **Well-designed surfaces don't just serve. They speak.** They communicate identity, guide behavior, and make public life more vibrant.

**Location:** Victoria, BC | **Installed by:** Square One Paving | **Products:** DecoMark® + StreetBond® | **Completed:** May 2024

This project combined advanced surface coatings with precision graphics to revitalize a key public area — blending functionality, safety, and community pride.

## StreetBond®: Durable Coatings with Visual Impact

StreetBond® is a high-performance coating system formulated to improve the durability and visual appeal of asphalt and concrete surfaces. Designed to perform under harsh conditions — including freeze-thaw cycles, UV exposure, and high-traffic wear — it's trusted by municipalities, planners, and contractors across North America.

**Key Benefits:**

- UV-stable, slip-resistant surface
- Custom color palette for civic branding
- Long-term performance without cracking or peeling

> **Where it shines:** Crosswalks, transit zones, bike lanes, park plazas, school zones, and community hubs

StreetBond lets cities express identity *through infrastructure* — without sacrificing durability or safety.

## DecoMark®: Urban Graphics with Staying Power

DecoMark® transforms pavement into communication. Whether you're guiding pedestrians, branding a public plaza, or creating a piece of urban art, DecoMark delivers precision surface graphics that hold up under time, weather, and traffic.

**Ideal For:**

- Wayfinding and directional icons
- Branded intersections or cultural markers
- School, park, and civic installations

> Think of it as **visual language, built into the street.**

DecoMark® is engineered for longevity, using thermoplastic materials designed to embed color and structure into the surface itself — reducing maintenance, improving clarity, and giving cities a reliable tool for storytelling.

## Design Benefits that Build Better Cities

### 1. Strengthen Civic Identity

Symbols, colors, and graphics reflect the values and stories of a place. With StreetBond and DecoMark, cities can communicate identity visually — reinforcing local pride and creating a sense of belonging.

### 2. Improve Safety & Navigation

Color-contrasted surfaces and clear markings reduce confusion and risk for pedestrians, cyclists, and drivers. Design can guide behavior — making public spaces safer and more intuitive.

### 3. Encourage Gathering and Interaction

Well-designed spaces invite people to linger, explore, and connect. Decorative paving turns passive areas into active environments that support community interaction.

### 4. Extend Surface Life

StreetBond's protective layer shields asphalt and concrete from water, UV rays, and abrasion — reducing the need for frequent resurfacing and lowering lifecycle costs.

## Addressing Common Questions

**Q: Does decorative paving wear out faster than traditional materials?**
No. StreetBond and DecoMark are engineered for long-term performance under demanding conditions — including snowplows, road salt, and freeze-thaw climates.

**Q: Is it just aesthetic?**
Not at all. While visually striking, these solutions also improve safety, extend pavement life, and enable functional communication (wayfinding, lane separation, etc.).

**Q: What's the ROI for cities?**
Improved safety, reduced maintenance costs, and increased public satisfaction. Aesthetic upgrades often increase tourism and community engagement, adding economic and social value.
